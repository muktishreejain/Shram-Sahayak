The Ministry of Labour and Employment's Labour Codes page lists the Code on Wages, Industrial Relations Code, Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, and Code on Social Security. It provides code documents, Central Rules, notifications, FAQs, and booklets. The page should be checked for current rules and notices before relying on a detail, because dates and amendments matter.

The supplied Compliance Handbook for Employers describes itself as a simplified reference for employers in the Central Government sphere. Its disclaimer says it is not a legal document and that the Labour Codes prevail if the handbook and legislation differ. The handbook is employer-focused; it is not an individual's employment contract, wage slip, or proof that a worker is eligible for a benefit. Rules for a state-sphere establishment may differ.

For a worker, the handbook can help identify records or questions to verify, such as wage slips, payment dates, attendance, safety arrangements, and final dues. It cannot by itself determine whether an employer broke the law or how much a worker is owed. Compare a document only with what is visibly stated, keep a copy, and ask an official labour service or qualified adviser to confirm uncertain points. For a wage or employment grievance, check the SAMADHAN portal and its current instructions.
