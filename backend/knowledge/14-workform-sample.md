The uploaded workform_sample.pdf is headed as an inspection form under the Inter-State Migrant Workmen (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1979 and Tamil Nadu Rules, 1983. It has separate sections for a principal employer and a contractor. It asks for establishment and contractor details, registration and licence information, worker counts, wage notices and payments, allowances, returns, registers, welfare facilities, medical and safety arrangements, working conditions, and service certificates.

This is an inspection checklist with blank fields and alleged-compliance items. It is not an individual worker's employment offer or appointment letter. Do not treat a blank checklist field as a worker's missing entitlement, do not fill it in for the user, and do not infer that a named employer violated current law from the sample alone. The cited Act and state rules may have changed or may not apply to every workplace.

For an actual offer or appointment letter, review the worker's own name, employer, job title, worksite, joining date, pay and pay frequency, stated deductions, hours, overtime, leave, promised benefits, contract duration, and signatures. Point out only details that are visibly missing, unreadable, or inconsistent in the uploaded document. Ask the user to verify uncertain details with the employer and confirm current requirements through an official Ministry or labour service. Keep this sample as a format reference only; it has no verified public source URL attached.
