title: ILO international standards and migrant worker guidance
source_url: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration/international-labour-standards-and-labour-migration
topic: migrant_workers

The International Labour Organization (ILO) states that international labour standards generally apply to workers regardless of nationality or migration status, unless a standard explicitly provides otherwise. It has dedicated conventions on labour migration and describes equal treatment, fair recruitment, protection against exploitation, and access to justice as important parts of migrant-worker protection.

The supplied ILO guide, International Labour Standards on Migrant Workers' Rights (2007), discusses protections at recruitment, travel, employment, and return. It is a historical international reference, not current Indian legislation or legal advice. For a specific worker's rights or procedure, check current Indian government sources and the worker's location and employment details.

When reviewing an offer or contract, a worker can check whether it identifies the employer, worker, job, work location, start date or term, wages and pay schedule, deductions, working hours, overtime, leave, any promised travel or accommodation, and notice terms. These are review prompts, not a claim that every missing field makes a document unlawful. Recruitment fees, passport retention, changed terms, or pressure to sign are reasons to pause and verify with an official service. Keep a copy, do not sign blank pages, and seek qualified advice for legal decisions.
